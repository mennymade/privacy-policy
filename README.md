# Privacy Policy for Fool's Decree

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026  
**Developer:** MennyMade  
**Application ID:** `com.foolsdecree.roguelike`  

---

## 1. Overview
**Fool's Decree** ("the Game") is a roguelike deckbuilding dice game developed and maintained by **MennyMade**. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play Fool's Decree on Android and mobile devices.

## 2. Information We Collect
Fool's Decree is engineered to be privacy-first:
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not require accounts, logins, or personal details (such as real name, email, phone number, or physical address).
- **Local Game State:** Your unlocked collars, monarchs, high scores, and cosmetics are stored locally on your device storage via standard device-level web/app storage.
- **Analytics & Crash Reports:** Standard anonymized diagnostic information (device model, Android version, crash stack traces) may be processed via Google Play Console services to ensure game performance and stability.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect standard mobile advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID - GAID) in accordance with Google Play policies to serve contextual or non-personalized advertisements.

## 3. Advertising
The game may feature optional rewarded video advertisements and rate-limited interstitial ads. Players who purchase the in-game **Sovereign Pass** receive a permanently ad-free experience, disabling all forced third-party ads.

## 4. In-App Purchases
All transactions are handled securely through **Google Play Billing**. We do not collect or store credit card numbers, payment tokens, or financial records.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Fool's Decree is directed toward a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
